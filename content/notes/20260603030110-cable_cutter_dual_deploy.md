When using a Cable Cutter Dual Deploy, the rocket ejects a parachute at apogee, but it is held closed by a zip tie. In this configuration, the parachute basically acts as a streamer. This combined with the tumbling of the rocket (due to the separated bay) should be enough to slow the rocket down.

Then at main event, the zip tie is cut and the parachute opens. This method is generally more space-efficient than the [Two Parachute Dual Deploy](@/notes/20260603030122-two_parachute_dual_deploy.md) method and does not require designing a rocket for dual deployment.

For this, we need to make or buy a [Flight Computer](@/notes/20260606203327-flight_computer.md). To cut the cable itself, something like a **pyrotechnic cable cutter** is sufficient. The basics of this is some black power is stored in some container, which is then ignited via a piston. The ignition then burns the zip tie and releases the parachute. See [Flight Computer](@/notes/20260606203327-flight_computer.md) for common igniters.
