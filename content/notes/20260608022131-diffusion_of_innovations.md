From <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion_of_innovations>

Five elements influence the spread of new ideas, which is highly dependent on social capital (which in a nutshell are "networks of relationships productive towards advancing the goals of individuals and groups"[^fn:1]. In other words, networking. The five elements are

1.  the innovation
2.  adopters
3.  communication channels
4.  time
5.  social system

The bigger point is that an idea or innovation can become self-sustainable with a large number of adopters. After it reaches what is called "critical mass", the idea is self-sustainable and can create more adopters. Apparently, this is theorized to be at the point between "early adopters and early majority"[^fn:2].
