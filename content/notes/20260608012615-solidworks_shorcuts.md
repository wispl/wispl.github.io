## Anywhere {#anywhere}

-   s: shows shortcuts (awesome)
-   d: shows confirmation and breadcrumbs (awesome)
-   Spacebar: orientation selector (awesome)
-   Ctrl-q: rebuild
-   z: zoom out
-   Z: zoom in
-   f: fit
-   g: magnifying glass


## Assembly {#assembly}

-   Alt-Click: show hidden part
-   Tab: show/hide components under pointer
-   Shift-Tab: show/hide all components under pointer
-   Ctrl-Shift-Tab: show all hidden components and lets you pick which one to show
-   Alt-drag: smart mate
-   Alt-Ctrl-Drag: copy and make smart mate
-   Ctrl-Drag: Copies
