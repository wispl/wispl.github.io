With this deployment, a smaller parachute (called the drogue parachute) is launched at apogee. Then at a specific height the apogee or main parachute is deployed. The height (generally around 500-1000 ft) must be selected such that the rocket is sufficiently slowed enough that the main parachute will not tear.

The rocket must be specifically designed for dual deployment. That is your rocket must have the following

-   space between the nose cone payload bay for the drogue parachute
-   space between the payload bay and motor area for the main parachute

Of course, you will also need an [Electronics Bay](@/notes/20260606213623-electronics_bay.md), [Flight Computer](@/notes/20260606203327-flight_computer.md), and all the basic stuff. Note that it doesn't matter which space the parachutes go in (drogue can be in the top or bottom space, same idea with main parachute).
