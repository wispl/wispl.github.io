To correctly gauge the pressure, the barometer inside the altimeter needs to have access to the outside air. So we need right sized holes to let the right amount of flow in, but not too large. Large holes have a change to trick the barometer due to the added and Bernoulli's Principle.

The general guideline is the area of the holes should be 0.05 in^2 for every 100 in^3 of payload bay area. And you want the positioning of the holes to be

1.  three or four holes spaced evenly around the tube
2.  at least two body tube diameters from the base of the nose cone

The following formulas can be used to find the diameter

\\[
V = \pi l r^2 + \pi r^2 \frac{h}{3} \quad 100A = 0.05V \quad A = \pi r^2
\\]

The first formula gives the volume of the payload bay given its length and radius. The second gives the relation between area of the hole and volume. And the third is the area of a circle, you know what to do with this one.
